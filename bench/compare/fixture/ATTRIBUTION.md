# Fixture sources

- `html/`: the English Wikipedia article
  ["Saint Petersburg"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg), saved
  2026-11-06 with its two skin stylesheets by `make_html.py`. Text and styles
  are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
  by Wikipedia's contributors. Changes: scripts removed, links pointed at `#`,
  every image replaced by a generated placeholder of the same size.
- `js/`: our own small React app in the style of the RealWorld "Conduit" demo
  (no code copied from it). `app.js` is the production bundle of `src/app.jsx`
  with React 19 (MIT); rebuild with `npm ci && npm run build` in `js/`.
- `heavy/`: composed for this benchmark; not a copy of a real site.
- Images are generated by `server.mjs`; fonts are the image's Liberation fonts
  (SIL OFL), served from `/usr/share/fonts`.
